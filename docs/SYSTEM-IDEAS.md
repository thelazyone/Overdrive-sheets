# System expansion shortlist

Proposals for discussion, not accepted rules, requirements, or printable systems. This is the place to develop ideas before promoting them into the presets and Charter data. The generated [current roster](SHIP-SYSTEMS.md) records what already exists.

Start with one-option families: Contemplation's Weapon and Engine; Providence's Docking Bay and Effectors; Salvation's Loading Bay, Hangar, and Cargo Storage. A single option is a useful design prompt, not proof that a slot needs more content.

| Ship / slot family | Proposed option | Role and tradeoff to explore | Possible Charter gate — not assigned |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contemplation / Weapon ×2 | Escort Flak Mount | A defensive alternative to the Asteroid Buster; trade mining capability for screening light ships. First check whether an existing flak design can be reused. | Escorting Rights or Extended Squadrons |
| Contemplation / Engine | Station-Keeping Drive | Better low-speed positioning for escort work, at the expense of transit speed. | Unassigned; assess whether it should be baseline |
| Providence / Docking Bay | Recovery Cradle | Specialize in handling salvage instead of general docking. Define a meaningful limitation before adding numerical bonuses. | Extraction Concession |
| Providence / Effectors | Precision Grappler | Close-range object handling as an alternative to Long Range Effectors. | Field Manipulators |
| Salvation / Loading Bay | Sorting Gantry | Improve a specific cargo-handling action while tying up crew; avoid a universal income multiplier. | Advanced Logistics or Mass Storage |
| Salvation / Hangar | Tug Service Bay | Support working tugboats in exchange for the current Skiffs Hangar role. | Advanced Logistics |
| Salvation / Cargo Storage | Specialized Cargo Hold | Favor one cargo task or type, with a restriction that makes general storage remain useful. | Mass Storage |
| Opulence / Weapon ×2 | Defensive Battery | Explore a screening alternative to mining/direct fire; reuse an existing weapon if its role and physical fit work. | Unassigned |

For each candidate, settle the action, crew/energy cost, limitation, requirements, compatible physical slot, and test question. Do not assign numerical effects from these descriptions alone.

Shared-slot rule: fore/aft copies of a weapon family and left/right copies of the same named family share the option list and access conditions. Show them once with a slot count; do not turn duplicated mounts into duplicated unlocks.

## Energy weapon roles to explore

The nine names and their Ion/Laser/EMP families were accepted for the Charter catalog on 2026-10-26. The distinctions below remain design proposals, not implemented effects or access requirements. Existing EMP Emitter on the Supply Tender retains its current profile and now links to the shared weapon identity.

| Family | Weapon | Proposed distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ion | Ion Beam | General-purpose baseline |
| Ion | Phased Ion Beam | Specialized shield interaction |
| Ion | Collimated Ion Beam | Longer reach with narrower targeting constraints |
| Laser | Laser Cannon | Reliable concentrated fire |
| Laser | Pulse Laser Cannon | Short bursts against small or fleeting targets |
| Laser | Adaptive Laser Cannon | Flexible targeting or firing modes, at an efficiency cost |
| EMP | EMP Emitter | Localized disruption |
| EMP | Focused EMP Emitter | Reach or precision against one system |
| EMP | Wideband EMP Emitter | Broader disruption with weaker individual effects |

## External Torpedo Mount

Name accepted 2026-10-26. A separate external attachment for torpedoes, distinct from internal ship systems and normal gun mounts. Compatible ships, mounting rules, payloads, stats, and Charter/trait requirements remain unspecified. Do not classify it as a Heavy gun or invent a fitting slot yet.

## Scatter Gun

Accepted name in Kinetic Weapons (2026-10-26). Explore area-hit behavior. Area shape, targeting, damage, costs, and access requirements are not yet defined; no area-hit mechanics have been implemented.
